Human review notice

People review the work. This page does not sign anyone in.

Zenzo’s public site explains how human review fits into agentic work. This page is informational only: it does not connect to a review queue, request credentials, or grant access to internal tools.

Do not enter credentials on a page that claims to be a Zenzo review portal.

Internal review records should be accessed only through an organization’s approved internal workflow. If an unexpected page asks you to sign in, verify the destination independently before doing anything.

What human review means

A person owns the decision.

Human review means the responsible person checks the relevant context, evaluates the output, and decides whether the next step is appropriate. A product interface can make work easier to inspect, but it does not replace accountable judgment.

  • Review the objective, source material, and output in context.
  • Validate a recommendation before a consequential action is taken.
  • Record feedback and decision context through approved team processes.
