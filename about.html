About Zenzo Beta

A calmer place to direct AI work.

Zenzo is a product workspace for teams that need to frame a goal, coordinate specialized AI agents, and keep the resulting work visible in one shared context. It is designed to make agentic work easier to review rather than harder to understand.

What the app is for

One direction in. A visible flow out.

The experience centers work around an active squad room: a goal in context, the specialists responding to it, and operational state that is easy to inspect. The product direction is deliberately practical rather than magical.

Agent orchestration

Organize specialists around a shared objective and keep handoffs visible instead of scattering work across disconnected prompts.

Shared context

Human review

People remain responsible for decisions.

Human review is part of responsible AI work. In Zenzo, it means a person reads the relevant output, checks the next action, and decides whether a task is ready to proceed.

  • Review happens in approved internal workflows, not through a public sign-in page.
  • Public visitors do not need a privileged account to understand how review works.
  • The staff review notice is non-authenticating and is not a login portal.
