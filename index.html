Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Zenzo and how does it differ from single-agent chat apps?
Zenzo is designed around specialized AI coworkers organized in channels and squads. Unlike standard chatbots, Zenzo agents have their own interactive sandboxed browser, live CDP action streaming, tool policy governance, and full human takeover capability.
How does the live browser and Take Control feature work?
Zenzo runs an isolated Chromium runtime. When an agent is directed to explore, research, or test web apps, you can watch DOM interactions in real-time. If human intervention is needed (e.g. solving auth challenges), you can toggle "Take Control" to directly click and type, then hand control back to the agent seamlessly.
Are there any fake tasks or dummy audit logs in Zenzo?
No. In Zenzo v0.2.1, all placeholder tasks and dummy audit logs were completely removed. All audit records and browser action logs are generated strictly from live runtime operations, CDP events, and actual tool executions.
How does Zenzo protect against social engineering or credential interception?
Zenzo never proxies, intercepts, or stores third-party login credentials. The early access portal only collects your name, email, and intended use-case directly into our private staff review queue. All simulated browser tasks run inside isolated sandboxes governed by POL-001 (Anti-Harvesting Policy), and live app sessions require explicit human operator takeover for authentication.
